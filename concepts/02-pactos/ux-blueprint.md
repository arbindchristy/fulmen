# UX Blueprint

## Core screens
- Deal portfolio
- Live negotiation workbench
- Clause graph and issue map
- Redline studio
- Approval waterfall and precedent explorer

## UX feel
- Dense, editorial, and decisive.
- More like a high-end trading terminal for contract leverage than a passive document system.
- AI should feel embedded in the work surface, not hidden in a side chat panel.

## Information architecture
- Portfolio by deal, vendor, renewal date, and approval risk
- Deal room with strategy panel, document panel, and decision log
- Clause-centric drill-down to precedent, fallback text, and impacted approvals
- Separate view for approved positions versus working drafts

## What makes the product feel premium and agent-native
- Split-pane document and strategy views that let users see text, rationale, and fallback paths at once.
- Clause dependency visualization so one concession’s downstream effects are obvious.
- Meeting prep and negotiation briefs generated from the live issue map.
- Clean approval handoffs that explain the exact business tradeoff, not just “please approve.”
