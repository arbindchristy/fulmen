# ICP

## Ideal customer profile
- Global enterprises with large contract volume, central procurement, and cross-functional review requirements.
- Strong fit for software-heavy enterprises, multinational services businesses, industrial procurement teams, and data-sensitive buyers.
- Sweet spot: organizations running thousands of vendor or partner negotiations per year with clear playbooks but inconsistent execution.

## Buyer
- Chief Procurement Officer
- VP Strategic Sourcing
- Head of Legal Operations
- General Counsel for procurement-heavy environments

## User
- Procurement managers
- In-house commercial counsel
- Vendor managers
- Finance approvers and business owners

## Where this lands first
- Vendor renewals and new vendor onboarding deals with recurring templates.
- Initial wedge: `MSA`, `DPA`, security addendum, and pricing schedule negotiations for software and outsourced services.
