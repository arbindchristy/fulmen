# PactOS

## One-line thesis
An AI-native commercial negotiation workbench that prepares positions, drafts redlines, models concession tradeoffs, and routes approval across procurement, legal, and finance.

## Why this matters now
- Enterprise negotiations are slower and more complex because pricing volatility, security requirements, data terms, and budget controls now collide in the same deal cycle.
- CLM systems store documents and route approvals, but they do not actively help teams think through strategy, fallback positions, and tradeoff discipline.
- Large language models are strong at clause interpretation and drafting, but enterprise deal teams still need governed playbooks, precedent control, and approval boundaries.
- Procurement and legal leaders are under pressure to move faster without giving away margin, leverage, or risk posture.
