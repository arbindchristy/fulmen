# MVP Scope

## What the MVP includes
- Contract ingestion for common commercial agreement formats
- Clause issue map with standard versus non-standard positions
- Negotiation workbench with redline suggestions and fallback packages
- Approval flow for commercial, legal, and security deviations
- Precedent explorer tied to prior approved outcomes

## What is deferred
- Outbound email automation
- Counterparty portal
- Signature orchestration
- Full ERP or spend-system integration
- Advanced benchmark pricing datasets

## 90-day buildable slice
- Focus on software procurement renewals where templates repeat and cross-functional review is common.
- Support `MSA`, `DPA`, and security addendum workflows.
- Limit approval logic to a clear playbook for price, liability, term, and data use deviations.
- Prove that one procurement and legal team can shorten cycle time while improving consistency.
