# Landing Page

## Homepage hero
PactOS gives procurement and legal teams an AI-native deal room for faster negotiations without uncontrolled concessions.

## 3 key value props
- Turn scattered redlines and tribal knowledge into a governed negotiation workbench.
- Draft stronger fallback positions and explain tradeoffs before approvals stall the deal.
- Preserve a clean decision trail across procurement, legal, finance, and security.

## Buyer-facing messaging
- Most contract cycles are slow because teams do not lack templates. They lack live strategic leverage and disciplined approval flow.
- PactOS combines clause reasoning, precedent memory, and delegated authority into one work surface.
- Agents help teams think, draft, and prepare. The system keeps approval, access, and audit fully controlled.

## Short pitch
PactOS is a premium negotiation workbench for enterprise procurement and legal teams. It helps teams negotiate faster and more consistently by turning contracts, playbooks, precedent, and approval policy into one AI-native operating surface.
