# ICP

## Ideal customer profile
- Global enterprises with large third-party ecosystems and mature security, privacy, or operational resilience programs.
- Best fit for financial institutions, healthcare networks, cloud-heavy technology companies, and large enterprises with outsourced critical services.
- Sweet spot: organizations assessing hundreds of suppliers per year and struggling to drive remediation to closure.

## Buyer
- CISO
- Head of Third-Party Risk Management
- Chief Procurement Risk Officer
- VP Security Assurance

## User
- Third-party risk analysts
- Vendor managers
- Supplier security and compliance leads
- Risk approvers and exception owners

## Where this lands first
- Post-assessment remediation management for cyber and privacy findings.
- Initial wedge: top 25 to 100 critical suppliers where unresolved findings carry clear business or regulatory exposure.
