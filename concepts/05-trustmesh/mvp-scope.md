# MVP Scope

## What the MVP includes
- Intake for third-party findings from one assessment source
- Secure supplier-facing case room
- Evidence review workspace with AI-generated completeness and quality checks
- Exception and approval workflow for remediation acceptance
- Portfolio dashboard for open gaps, aging, and supplier concentration

## What is deferred
- Continuous attack-surface intelligence feeds
- Broad benchmark datasets across industries
- Automated contract enforcement actions
- Deep integration with every major TPRM platform
- Full supplier performance scoring beyond remediation

## 90-day buildable slice
- Focus on cybersecurity findings for the top 25 critical suppliers.
- Support evidence request, supplier submission, analyst review, and exception approval.
- Limit integrations to one assessment source and basic identity/email plumbing.
- Prove faster closure cycles and cleaner residual-risk documentation.
