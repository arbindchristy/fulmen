# UX Blueprint

## Core screens
- Portfolio risk dashboard
- Supplier case room
- Evidence review and diff view
- Exception board
- Supplier communications timeline

## UX feel
- High-trust, bilateral, and procedural.
- The UI should feel like a secure case management product, not a generic portal.
- Every shared artifact should make boundary, status, and ownership obvious.

## Information architecture
- Portfolio by supplier criticality, finding severity, and aging
- Case room with separate enterprise and supplier actions on one timeline
- Evidence review that distinguishes raw submission from AI interpretation
- Exception views that expose decision rationale and expiry dates

## What makes the product feel premium and agent-native
- Supplier submissions are instantly turned into reviewable evidence packets instead of loose attachments.
- Analysts can see freshness, reuse, and insufficiency signals without manual reading first.
- Shared workspaces stay structured enough for collaboration but controlled enough for assurance teams.
- Portfolio pattern views show recurring supplier weaknesses, not just individual findings.
