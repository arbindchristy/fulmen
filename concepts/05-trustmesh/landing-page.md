# Landing Page

## Homepage hero
TrustMesh turns third-party findings into governed remediation closure instead of endless chasing and weak exception files.

## 3 key value props
- Give enterprise and supplier teams one secure remediation case room with clear ownership.
- Review supplier evidence faster without lowering assurance standards.
- Preserve a clean audit trail for every exception, deadline, and closure decision.

## Buyer-facing messaging
- Most third-party risk platforms stop at scoring. The expensive work starts after the assessment.
- TrustMesh is built for the remediation trench where suppliers submit partial proof, deadlines slip, and analysts need defensible decisions fast.
- Agents reduce review drag. The system keeps access, approval, and closure authority under control.

## Short pitch
TrustMesh is a remediation operations product for third-party risk teams. It helps enterprises and suppliers close material findings faster by combining secure collaboration, AI-native evidence review, and governed exception handling.
