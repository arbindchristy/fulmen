# TrustMesh

## One-line thesis
A networked remediation cockpit where enterprises and their critical suppliers jointly close third-party assurance gaps with evidence, deadlines, and governed exception decisions.

## Why this matters now
- Third-party risk programs generate more findings than teams can realistically close, especially across global supplier bases.
- Questionnaire-first tooling captures assessment data but usually fails at the messy remediation phase where real risk is reduced.
- Supplier collaboration still happens in email and file exchanges with poor traceability, which drags closure cycles and weakens accountability.
- AI can now normalize evidence, summarize remediation status, and detect portfolio patterns, but the workflow still requires strict tenant boundaries, approvals, and auditability.
