# ICP

## Ideal customer profile
- Global enterprises with high-value, high-volume operational exceptions and measurable service or margin impact.
- Best fit for manufacturers, distributors, logistics-heavy retailers, industrial service providers, and B2B operations teams with multi-system exception queues.
- Sweet spot: organizations with centralized operations centers but inconsistent local decision quality.

## Buyer
- COO
- Chief Supply Chain Officer
- VP Operations
- Head of Customer Operations in service-heavy organizations

## User
- Exception managers
- Planners and dispatch leads
- Customer operations supervisors
- Finance or service approvers for credits, expedite costs, or route changes

## Where this lands first
- One exception-heavy workflow where the economic tradeoff is clear.
- Initial wedge: delayed fulfillment, stockout recovery, missed service commitments, shipment reroutes, or urgent customer order recovery.
