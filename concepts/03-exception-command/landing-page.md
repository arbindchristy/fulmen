# Landing Page

## Homepage hero
Exception Command helps operations teams resolve the right exception the right way before service failures become margin leaks.

## 3 key value props
- Turn fragmented exception data into one governed decision packet.
- Compare recovery options by service impact, cost, and approval risk in seconds.
- Standardize operational judgment without forcing full automation.

## Buyer-facing messaging
- Visibility is not the problem anymore. Decision quality at scale is.
- Exception Command gives operators ranked options and gives leaders confidence that thresholds and approvals are still enforced.
- Agents assemble the case and model the options. The system controls what can actually happen.

## Short pitch
Exception Command is a decision-intelligence product for operations leaders. It helps distributed teams make faster, more consistent exception decisions by combining cross-system case assembly, option modeling, and governed approval flow.
