# MVP Scope

## What the MVP includes
- Unified exception inbox for one operational workflow
- Agent-generated case files with structured impact fields
- Ranked option comparison with estimated tradeoffs
- Approval routing for above-threshold decisions
- Outcome capture and basic recommendation feedback loop

## What is deferred
- Full autonomous execution
- Broad multi-workflow coverage across every ops domain
- Advanced digital twin simulation
- Predictive forecasting before exceptions happen
- Real-time optimization across the entire network

## 90-day buildable slice
- Focus on delayed order recovery for one operations team.
- Integrate three systems such as `ERP`, `CRM`, and transportation or service dispatch data.
- Support a narrow set of recommended actions with clear approval thresholds.
- Measure faster resolution time, lower manual escalations, and better service-versus-cost consistency.
