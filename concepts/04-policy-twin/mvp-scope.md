# MVP Scope

## What the MVP includes
- Policy document ingestion with version comparison
- Obligation extraction and structured review
- Impact mapping workspace for processes, systems, and control owners
- Change packet generation with approval routing
- Implementation tracking linked back to source obligations

## What is deferred
- Automated regulatory monitoring feeds
- Full multilingual coverage
- Auto-generated training content for every impacted team
- Real-time control testing
- Deep industry-specific legal content libraries

## 90-day buildable slice
- Focus on one internal policy domain such as security or data handling.
- Support policy version comparison and rollout across three departments.
- Provide obligation review, owner assignment, approval flow, and evidence capture for 30 to 50 obligations.
- Prove that one policy update can move from source text to approved implementation plan in days, not weeks.
