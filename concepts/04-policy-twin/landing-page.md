# Landing Page

## Homepage hero
Policy Twin turns dense policy change into approved operational action instead of another round of workshops and spreadsheets.

## 3 key value props
- Translate policy text into obligations, owners, controls, and deadlines automatically.
- Route control changes and exceptions through governed approvals with full traceability.
- Give leadership proof that policy is actually being implemented, not just acknowledged.

## Buyer-facing messaging
- Most enterprises can publish policy quickly. Very few can operationalize it cleanly.
- Policy Twin creates a living operational twin of your policy estate so risk, compliance, and operations work from the same implementation model.
- Agents perform the translation work. The system preserves authority, versioning, approval, and auditability.

## Short pitch
Policy Twin is a category-defining product for policy operations. It converts policy and regulatory change into mapped operational action, governed approvals, and evidence-backed implementation status across the enterprise.
