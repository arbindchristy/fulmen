# Workflow

## End-to-end user workflow
1. A team ingests a new or revised policy, regulation, or customer commitment.
2. The Obligation Extractor Agent identifies operative requirements, deadlines, scope, and ambiguities.
3. The Impact Mapper Agent links those obligations to known processes, systems, control owners, and affected teams.
4. The Change Planner Agent drafts implementation tasks, control updates, approvals, and evidence expectations.
5. The Conflict Finder Agent flags clashes with current policy, existing controls, or operating constraints.
6. Humans confirm applicability, assign owners, and refine the rollout plan.
7. The system routes material control changes or high-risk exceptions through formal approval paths.
8. Progress, evidence, and approvals stay linked back to the original policy text and version.

## Where agents are used
- Obligation extraction
- Impact mapping across systems and controls
- Change packet drafting
- Conflict and gap detection

## Where system controls are used
- Policy versioning and source-of-truth storage
- Approval routing for control changes and exceptions
- Ownership assignment and segregation-of-duties checks
- Audit logging across the full policy-to-implementation chain

## Where human decisions remain
- Confirming legal or operational applicability
- Approving control design and implementation plans
- Accepting exceptions, compensating controls, or rollout timing
- Declaring a policy change fully implemented
