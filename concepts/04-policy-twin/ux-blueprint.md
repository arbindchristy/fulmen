# UX Blueprint

## Core screens
- Policy intake and diff viewer
- Obligation review table
- Impact graph across processes, systems, and owners
- Rollout board with approvals
- Traceability timeline from source text to completed action

## UX feel
- Serious, traceable, and executive-ready.
- It should feel like a control-room for policy change, not a document summarizer.
- The interface should reward structured review and make ambiguity obvious.

## Information architecture
- Portfolio by policy domain, version, status, and business area
- Source-text-first navigation into obligations and downstream impacts
- Dedicated views for open ambiguities, conflicts, and pending approvals
- Completion views that show evidence and unresolved exceptions

## What makes the product feel premium and agent-native
- Source text stays visible next to extracted obligations and mapped impacts.
- The user can jump from one sentence in a policy to the exact tasks, owners, and evidence it created.
- Conflict visualization shows where policies collide or leave ownership gaps.
- Approval flows explain what changed operationally, not just what document was updated.
