# ICP

## Ideal customer profile
- Global enterprises with complex internal policy estates and frequent cross-functional control changes.
- Strong fit for financial services, healthcare, energy, telecom, critical infrastructure, and large technology companies with heavy governance obligations.
- Sweet spot: organizations where policy, risk, compliance, and operations teams all share responsibility but lack a common implementation surface.

## Buyer
- Chief Risk Officer
- Chief Compliance Officer
- COO
- CISO for policy domains tied to security or data handling

## User
- Policy operations managers
- Control owners
- Business process owners
- IT and security change leads
- Internal audit or assurance teams

## Where this lands first
- Internal policy rollouts and externally triggered regulatory response programs.
- Initial wedge: data handling, access governance, incident response, third-party risk, or business continuity policy changes.
