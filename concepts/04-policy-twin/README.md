# Policy Twin

## One-line thesis
An AI-native policy-to-operation system that translates new regulations and internal policies into impacted processes, required controls, implementation plans, and approval-ready change packets.

## Why this matters now
- Regulatory change, customer commitments, and internal policy updates are increasing faster than enterprises can operationalize them.
- Most policy teams still publish documents and spreadsheets, then rely on operating teams to interpret what changed.
- The gap between “policy exists” and “policy is actually implemented” is where audit failures, operational inconsistency, and executive risk accumulate.
- LLMs are finally useful for decomposing dense policy text, but enterprises need system-enforced ownership, approval, and traceability to trust the result.
