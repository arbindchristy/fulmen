# MVP Scope

## What the MVP includes
- One governed evidence cycle for a selected control family
- Connectors for three common systems such as identity, cloud, and ticketing
- A control workspace with artifact grouping, gap detection, and narrative drafting
- Reviewer approval flow for evidence acceptance and exception handling
- Audit-ready export with provenance and decision history

## What is deferred
- Full continuous controls monitoring
- External auditor portal
- Automated remediation execution
- Industry-specific control content libraries at broad scale
- Benchmarking across customers

## 90-day buildable slice
- Focus on security and IT general controls for a single enterprise team.
- Support 20 to 30 recurring controls.
- Ship connectors for `Okta`, `AWS`, and `Jira` or equivalent common systems.
- Prove one monthly evidence cycle from collection through approval-ready pack generation.
