# Landing Page

## Homepage hero
ControlProof turns audit evidence from a quarterly scramble into a governed, always-ready proof system.

## 3 key value props
- Assemble evidence across cloud, identity, tickets, and manual approvals without spreadsheet chasing.
- Detect weak proof and contradictory artifacts before auditors do.
- Produce approval-ready, provenance-backed audit packs that teams can trust.

## Buyer-facing messaging
- Your controls are not failing because your team lacks software. They are failing because proof is fragmented, stale, and manually assembled.
- ControlProof gives compliance and audit teams a governed evidence factory, not another registry.
- Agents do the evidence assembly work. System controls preserve trust, lineage, and approval discipline.

## Short pitch
ControlProof is an AI-native evidence operations product for regulated enterprises. It continuously builds defensible control proof from messy operational artifacts and routes exceptions through system-enforced review before anything is signed off.
