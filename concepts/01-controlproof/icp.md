# ICP

## Ideal customer profile
- Global enterprises with mature internal controls and recurring audit pressure.
- Best fit industries: financial services, healthcare, pharma, telecom, critical infrastructure, and large public companies with complex IT estates.
- Sweet spot: companies with 50 to 500 in internal audit, security compliance, or control ownership roles and 10 or more major control domains.

## Buyer
- Chief Compliance Officer
- Head of Internal Audit
- CISO
- VP GRC

## User
- Control owners
- Internal audit managers
- Security compliance analysts
- IT evidence coordinators

## Where this lands first
- IT general controls and security controls where evidence is highly repetitive, cross-system, and time-sensitive.
- Initial wedge: user access reviews, privileged access controls, change-management controls, vulnerability remediation controls, and backup or recovery controls.
