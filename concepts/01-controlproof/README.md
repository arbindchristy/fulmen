# ControlProof

## One-line thesis
An AI-native control evidence operating system that continuously assembles, validates, and routes audit-ready proof before auditors ask for it.

## Why this matters now
- Regulated enterprises run controls across cloud, SaaS, ticketing, identity, and manual approvals, but evidence still gets stitched together in spreadsheets and shared drives.
- Audit teams are being asked to cover more control surface area without proportional headcount growth.
- Generative AI is finally good at turning fragmented logs, screenshots, tickets, policies, and narratives into coherent evidence packs, but only if system controls enforce provenance, access, and approval.
- The market is full of GRC record systems and task trackers; very few products actively do the hard evidence assembly work.
