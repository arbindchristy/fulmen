# Recommendation

## Top 2 concepts to build first

### 1. ControlProof
- It has the cleanest overlap with Fulmen’s current strengths: governed workflows, approvals, auditability, and bounded agent roles.
- The pain is immediate, budgeted, and easy to explain to buyers without inventing a new category from scratch.
- The MVP can be narrow but still valuable because even one evidence-heavy control family creates measurable time savings and better audit readiness.

### 2. Policy Twin
- It is the boldest concept in the set and has the best chance to become a category-defining control-plane product instead of a feature.
- It directly leverages Fulmen’s “agents think, systems enforce, humans approve” model because policy translation is exactly where agent reasoning is valuable but unsafe without system control.
- It has strong executive appeal because it addresses a board-visible problem: the gap between policy intent and operating reality.

## Why these two
- Both concepts turn governed agentic reasoning into core workflow value rather than surface-level assistance.
- Both reward strong policy, approval, audit, and traceability infrastructure, which is where Fulmen is differentiated.
- Both can start narrow and still look serious to enterprise buyers.

## Which one is safest
- `ControlProof` is the safest.
- The buyer pain is already budgeted under audit, compliance, and control operations.
- The first wedge can be tightly scoped to one control family and a few integrations without losing credibility.

## Which one is boldest
- `Policy Twin` is the boldest.
- If executed well, it creates a new system of record for policy-to-operation change instead of competing head-on in a mature category.
- It also carries more category-creation risk because the first use case has to feel concrete immediately.

## What we should build in 30 days if we want to move fast
- Build a `ControlProof` alpha, not a broad platform.
- Scope it to one monthly evidence cycle for 10 to 15 controls in one domain such as access governance or change management.
- Include three source connectors, an evidence workspace, gap detection, reviewer approval, and provenance-backed export.
- Success metric: one design partner can complete an evidence cycle materially faster with a cleaner review trail than their current spreadsheet and ticket process.

## Practical sequencing
1. Build `ControlProof` first to validate the governed evidence and approval core.
2. Use the resulting workflow, provenance, and traceability primitives as the foundation for `Policy Twin`.
3. Revisit `PactOS` as the strongest commercial workbench alternate if buyer pull emerges outside regulated control domains.
